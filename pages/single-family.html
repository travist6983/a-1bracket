Scope

What we take on.

  • Brick veneer and thin brick

    Full facades, accent walls, and matches to existing brick.

  • Brick pointing

    Failed joints cut out and repointed to match the original mortar.

  • Natural and cultured stone

    Water tables, entries, and full elevations in real or cast stone.

  • Stucco

    Hard coat and Styrofoam base systems, new work and recoats.

  • Chimneys and fireplaces

    Rebuilds, repairs, and new construction, inside and out.

  • Retaining walls

    Structural walls that hold grade and look like they belong.

  • Steps and patios

    Brick, stone, and paver work set for drainage and freeze cycles.

  • Home additions

    Masonry for additions matched so the new work reads as original.

  • Caulking

    Joints sealed against water where masonry meets everything else.

Process

How a project runs.

  1. 01

    Walk the job

    We look at the work in person and give you a free written estimate, specific about scope.

  2. 02

    Set the schedule

    Material ordered through suppliers we have worked with for decades, dates set and kept.

  3. 03

    Build

    Licensed, insured crews on site, working clean and leaving the property orderly each day.

  4. 04

    Walk through

    We walk the finished work with you and close every item before we call it done.
