Since 1996

No job too big. No job too small.

A-1 Bracket Group, Inc. is a licensed masonry construction contractor company that specializes in a variety of brick, stucco, and stone masonry work. We have been helping Pennsylvania and New Jersey clients since we were established in 1996, and we strive to always provide our clients with professional service and outstanding results. Our clients' overall satisfaction is critical to our success and is the reason why we continue to grow.

No matter what type of masonry work you want completed, A-1 Bracket Group, Inc. is the contractor you have been seeking. Our team is composed of talented individuals who are detail oriented and perfectionists, which allows every project to look its best and be sustainable for many years to follow. Our team has everything they need to accomplish your vision, regardless of size or deadlines, and will work directly with you every step of the way to make it happen.

  • High-quality workmanship and services completed on time

  • State of the art equipment available to stay on track

  • Excellent relationships with our suppliers for quick deliveries

  • Abides by all safety and state regulations and upkeeps all licenses needed

We offer free estimates, and we are fully licensed and insured.

Our company believes in working together to accomplish your goals. A-1 Bracket Group, Inc. is proud to work with contractors, consultants, architects, homeowners, and business owners on any type of project, on any scale. We have not encountered a job yet where it was too big or too small for our team to work on. Another benefit of using us for all your masonry needs is that we are flexible when it comes to working with extension teams, which can be challenging to find. Bottom line, we understand that your project may require some additional help from people you trust and want overseeing the work, and we respect that.

Being dependable can mean many things, but A-1 Bracket wants to be more than just dependable to you — we want to be the obvious choice when you need masonry work because of our customer service, flexibility, experience, work ethic, and our smooth job process. Each project should leave you in awe, and we are confident in making that happen.

All work is completed in a neat, clean, and professional manner. From consultation to completion, we take the extra time to make sure each job is done right.

Our customers are our top priority, and you can expect us to go the extra mile to meet your specific needs and requirements.
