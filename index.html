About

Morrisville, PA. Since 1996.

A-1 Bracket Group, Inc. is a licensed and bonded masonry construction contractor specializing in brick, stucco, and stone. We started in 1996 as A-1 Bracket, Inc., became A-1 Bracket Group, Inc. in 2006, and have served clients in Pennsylvania and New Jersey the entire time.

Our team is detail oriented and works directly with the client at every step, on projects of any size. We work with contractors, consultants, architects, homeowners, and business owners, and we are glad to work alongside the other trades and teams you already trust.
